# The stages of Swarm. Lines are words separated by spaces; "#" starts a comment.
#
# A path is a curve in the field's coordinates (640 × 400, y downwards):
#   path <name>
#   from <x> <y>                                          where it starts
#   curve <c1x> <c1y> <c2x> <c2y> <x> <y>                 a Bézier curve to x, y, bent by two control points
#   line <x> <y>                                          a straight line
#   mirror <name> <other path>                            the other path, mirrored left to right
#
# A stage is a list of groups. A group flies in along one path, one insect after the other:
#   stage [challenge]
#   group <seconds into the stage> <path> <insect> <insect> ...
# An insect is bee, butterfly or boss, with its slot in the formation: bee@4,3 is column 4 (0 to 9 from the
# left), row 3 (0 to 4 from the top). An insect without a slot flies through and leaves; a challenge stage
# has only such insects, and a bonus for hitting all of them.
#
# After the last stage, the first comes again, a little faster each time.

# From the top: down and out to the side, round a loop at the bottom, and up to the formation.
path top-left
from 290 -16
curve 290 120  120 170  110 250
curve 100 330  250 340  260 260
curve 268 200  230 170  200 150
mirror top-right top-left

# From the lower side: in low over the fighter, up and round a loop below the formation.

# Challenge paths: through the field and out again.
